Cybersecurity and Legal Protection

Cybersecurity is essential in protecting sensitive digital assets from threats like hacking, phishing, and ransomware. Organizations must implement secure authentication and data protection strategies.

Legal frameworks such as GDPR and India’s IT Act ensure responsible data handling. Non-compliance can lead to penalties and reputational damage.

Best practices include strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, regular audits, and secure DevSecOps pipelines.
